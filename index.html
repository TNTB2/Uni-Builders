What construction services does UniBuilders offer?+

UniBuilders offers new home construction, renovations, additions, building repairs and property improvements. Services also include roofing, plumbing, paving, drywall, ceilings, boundary walls and general maintenance.

Can you renovate or extend an existing home?+

Yes. We handle renovations and additions, including interior improvements and structural work within an agreed scope. Existing conditions, access and your intended changes should be reviewed before a quote is finalised.

Do you handle building repairs and smaller improvements?+

Yes. Building repairs and general maintenance are part of our services alongside larger projects. Describe what needs attention and where the property is located so we can discuss a suitable next step.

Which areas do you serve?+

We serve Strand, Somerset West, Gordon’s Bay, Stellenbosch, Grabouw, Hermanus, Franschhoek, Paarl, Betty’s Bay, Pringle Bay, Rondebosch and Kuils River. Confirm your project location when enquiring.

How do I request a construction or renovation quote?+

Use the website enquiry form, call 073 984 7718 or email hello@unibuilders.co.za. Include your name, project area and a description of the construction, renovation or repair work you have in mind.

What information should I prepare for a quote?+

Prepare the property location, a short description of the work, approximate dimensions and any drawings or specifications you already have. Mention access restrictions and the project timing you would like to discuss.

How long does a renovation or new build take?+

Timing depends on the work, site conditions, materials, approvals and specialist coordination. Discuss a proposed programme and how changes will be handled rather than relying on a fixed general timescale.

Where can I view the NHBRC certificate?+

The original NHBRC certificate is displayed on the homepage with a full-size document link. It shows registration number 4000020889, issued 20 October 2025 with a printed expiry of 20 October 2026; ask the team for current registration confirmation.
