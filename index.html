BEFORE WE BEGIN

A few useful
answers.

Can I request a quote for a smaller project?+

Yes. Alongside new builds, we handle renovations, repairs and property improvements. Share the work you have in mind and the project location.

What should I include in my enquiry?+

Your location, a short description of the work and any plans or measurements you already have. You can email supporting photographs and documents after the first enquiry.

Can you work with my plans and material choices?+

We can discuss existing plans and your specifications, explain practical considerations and review alternatives with you before agreeing the work.

How do we agree the timing and documentation?+

Discuss the proposed programme, contract, approvals and project documentation directly with the team. These depend on the scope and should be confirmed for your project.
