Services & project types

What construction services does UniBuilders offer?+

UniBuilders offers new home construction, renovations, additions, building repairs and property improvements. Services also include roofing, plumbing, paving, drywall, ceilings, boundary walls and general maintenance.

Do you build new homes?+

Yes. We can discuss a residential construction project around your site, plans and specifications. Share your location, available drawings and the stage your planning has reached.

Can you renovate or extend an existing home?+

Yes. We handle renovations and additions, including interior improvements and structural work within an agreed scope. Existing conditions, access and your intended changes should be reviewed before a quote is finalised.

Do you handle building repairs and smaller improvements?+

Yes. Building repairs and general maintenance are part of our services alongside larger projects. Describe what needs attention and where the property is located so we can discuss a suitable next step.

Repairs & specialist work

Can you help with roofing repairs?+

Roofing is part of our service range. Tell us about the roof type, the problem you have noticed and any access concerns; the work and approach need to be assessed for the property.

Do you offer plumbing work?+

Yes. Plumbing work is coordinated with specialist subcontractors. Explain the installation or repair you need so the appropriate scope can be discussed.

Can you install drywall and ceilings?+

Yes. Drywall and ceiling installation are included in our property improvement services. Useful details include the room dimensions, intended layout, finish and any existing damage.

Do you build boundary walls or handle paving?+

Yes. Boundary walls and paving are included in our services. Share the approximate area, site access and what you would like to achieve so the requirements can be reviewed.

Location & service areas

Where is UniBuilders based?+

UniBuilders is based in Somerset West in the Western Cape. Our published contact address is Kim Street, Somerset West, Cape Town, 7130; please contact the team to arrange a visit.

Which areas do you serve?+

We serve Strand, Somerset West, Gordon’s Bay, Stellenbosch, Grabouw, Hermanus, Franschhoek, Paarl, Betty’s Bay, Pringle Bay, Rondebosch and Kuils River. Confirm your project location when enquiring.

Do you work in Somerset West and Gordon’s Bay?+

Yes. Somerset West and Gordon’s Bay are included in our service areas, together with Strand and the wider Helderberg region. Share the address or suburb when discussing your project.

Can I visit your office?+

Please contact UniBuilders before visiting to confirm arrangements. The website includes the business’s Google Maps location, contact number and published office hours.

Quotes & project preparation

How do I request a construction or renovation quote?+

Use the website enquiry form, call 073 984 7718 or email hello@unibuilders.co.za. Include your name, project area and a description of the construction, renovation or repair work you have in mind.

What information should I prepare for a quote?+

Prepare the property location, a short description of the work, approximate dimensions and any drawings or specifications you already have. Mention access restrictions and the project timing you would like to discuss.

Can I send photographs or building plans?+

Yes. Email supporting photographs, plans and measurements to hello@unibuilders.co.za when discussing your enquiry. Avoid sending information unrelated to the project.

How much will my building project cost?+

Cost depends on the agreed scope, site conditions, materials, access and specialist work. A project-specific quote is more useful than a generic rate; discuss what is included and any allowances before agreeing the work.

Planning, scope & communication

Can you work with my existing building plans?+

We can review your existing plans and discuss the construction work around them. Confirm any professional design work, approvals and responsibilities that apply to your project before work begins.

Can you help with estimates and bills of quantities?+

Yes. Estimates and bills of quantities can be discussed as part of project planning. The available drawings, specifications and level of detail will affect the scope of this work.

How long does a renovation or new build take?+

Timing depends on the work, site conditions, materials, approvals and specialist coordination. Discuss a proposed programme and how changes will be handled rather than relying on a fixed general timescale.

How will we communicate during the build?+

Agree a communication plan with the team before the project begins. Regular site conversations can be used to review progress, decisions and any changes to the agreed work.

Documentation & getting in touch

Where can I view the NHBRC certificate?+

The original NHBRC certificate is displayed on the homepage with a full-size document link. It shows registration number 4000020889, issued 20 October 2025 with a printed expiry of 20 October 2026; ask the team for current registration confirmation.

What should be confirmed before building work starts?+

Confirm the scope, specifications, quote, proposed timing, responsibilities and project documentation with the team. Discuss applicable approvals and specialist sign-offs with the responsible professionals for your project.

What are your opening hours?+

Published hours are Monday to Thursday, 07:00–17:30; Friday, 07:00–15:30; and Saturday, 08:00–14:00. UniBuilders is closed on Sundays. Contact the team to arrange a visit or project discussion.

What is the best way to contact UniBuilders?+

Call 073 984 7718, email hello@unibuilders.co.za or complete the website project enquiry form. On a phone, the persistent contact bar gives you direct call and quote links.
