Repair work starts with understanding what needs attention and the conditions around it. UniBuilders handles building repairs and general maintenance alongside larger construction and renovation projects. Explain what you have noticed, where it occurs and the work you would like to discuss so the requirements can be reviewed.

Useful information for a repair enquiry.

Send a short description, the location and photographs that show the affected area. Mention access constraints and whether you know when the issue began. The approach, materials and extent of the repair should be confirmed after reviewing the property; describe any uncertainty rather than assuming a repair method before assessment.
